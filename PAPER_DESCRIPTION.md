# Paper description

## Manuscript

**Title:** *Increasing the threonine-to-lysine ratio improves the growth
performance of slow-growing pigs within group precision feeding*

**Authors:** Pedro Righetti Arnaut, Candido Pomar, Luciano Hauschild, and Aline
Remus  
**Journal:** *Journal of Animal Science*  
**Status:** Submitted  
**Year:** 2026

## Overview

This manuscript addresses growth variability among pigs managed with group
precision feeding and investigates the response of slow-growing animals to
increasing dietary threonine-to-lysine ratios. It considers how pre-existing
growth patterns relate to later performance and evaluates growth responses at
the individual-animal level.

The associated reproducible analyses examine three principal questions:

1. Whether Fast, Medium, and Slow growth-rate categories are also explained by
   differences in birth weight.
2. How each animal's average daily gain changes from the pre-treatment period
   to the experimental feeding period.
3. What the measured growth rates would imply for the projected time required
   to reach a 120 kg market weight if those rates continued unchanged.

The analysis also describes the timing of body-weight differences among
slow-growing pigs, estimates uncertainty in the market-weight projections by
bootstrap resampling, presents projected growth trajectories, and compares
feed costs across feeding treatments.

The projection to 120 kg is an extrapolation rather than a directly observed
outcome. Feed-cost calculations use 2025 reference ingredient prices and are
intended for comparisons among treatments.

## Feeding treatments represented in the code

- `CPF100`: conventional phase feeding at 100% of the ideal
  threonine-to-lysine ratio of 0.65;
- `GPF80`: group precision feeding at 80% of the ideal ratio;
- `GPF100`: group precision feeding at 100% of the ideal ratio;
- `GPF120`: group precision feeding at 120% of the ideal ratio; and
- `GPF140`: group precision feeding at 140% of the ideal ratio.
